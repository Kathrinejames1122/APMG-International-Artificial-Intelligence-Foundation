A dedicated study guide and knowledge reference for candidates preparing for the **APMG BCS Artificial Intelligence (AI) Foundation Certification**, covering AI principles, ethical considerations, machine learning, robotics, and human-machine collaboration.

---

## Exam Overview

The APMG AI Foundation certification validates foundational knowledge of artificial intelligence, its capabilities and implications, and how AI products and services can be assessed from different perspectives.

- **Exam Format**: Closed-book examination; APMG identifies the Foundation assessment as a multiple-choice exam.
- **Target Audience**: Professionals and individuals seeking to understand AI and its potential role in organisations, including those in technology, business, science, engineering, and management.
- **Prerequisites**: No specific prior qualification is identified in the published Foundation information; self-study is available, although accredited training is recommended.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Ethical and Sustainable Human and Artificial Intelligence

- Understand ethical considerations in the development and use of AI.
- Explore sustainable and responsible approaches to human and artificial intelligence.
- Recognise the importance of human oversight and accountability in AI applications.

### 2. Artificial Intelligence and Robotics

- Understand the relationship between artificial intelligence and robotics.
- Explore how AI capabilities support automated and intelligent systems.
- Recognise the implications of AI-enabled technologies across different sectors.

### 3. Applying the Benefits of AI: Challenges and Risks

- Identify potential benefits and organisational applications of AI.
- Understand challenges and risks associated with AI implementation.
- Consider the implications of AI adoption for people, organisations, and society.

### 4. Building a Machine Learning Toolbox: Theory and Practice

- Understand the foundations and basic concepts of machine learning.
- Explore the processes and tools involved in developing machine learning capabilities.
- Recognise practical considerations when assessing AI products and services.

### 5. The Management, Role and Responsibilities of Humans and Machines

- Understand the changing relationship between people and AI systems.
- Explore human and machine roles in workplace activities and decision-making.
- Recognise the importance of human responsibilities when using AI.
